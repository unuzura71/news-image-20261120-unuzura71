**The World in a Day** — one image that sums up the world news of Friday, 20 November 2026,
drawn as a vintage newspaper front page. This is the final, full-day edition, replacing the
morning edition with the day's biggest stories as they stood by evening. The day's front page,
as this entry tells it:

- **The Hague** — the International Court of Justice ruled 9–6 that Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya, the first time the court has held a state responsible
  for genocide.
- **Shenzhen** — Trump, Xi and Putin held their first three-way summit and signed a
  four-point peace declaration on nuclear risk, UN reform and energy; Ukraine and Iran
  were not mentioned.
- **The Gulf** — Iran stepped up tanker attacks in the Strait of Hormuz; a tanker hit a mine
  and caught fire, and the IEA called the crisis the greatest threat ever observed to
  energy security.
- **Antalya** — COP31 ran past its Friday deadline after the draft deal dropped every
  mention of fossil fuels and the $120bn adaptation target.
- **Kyiv** — a fresh missile attack hit the capital on Friday after Ukraine struck the
  Kerch Bridge for the third time in a week; allies met in Berlin.
- **India** — Cyclone Akvan made landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha, the third cyclone in a
  month; 47,000 people had been evacuated.
- **Markets** — the AI sell-off deepened: Japan's Nikkei fell 4.8% and the S&P 500 lost 2%.

The repository holds the picture as `entry.png` and a small Node program, `main.ts`, that
copies it, byte for byte, to the output folder — no network, no dependencies, the same
bytes on every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

That writes `out/entry.png`, the image the judge sees.
